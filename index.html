About the company

A real estate acquisition company based in Ohio.

DAL Ohio LLC is a privately held real estate acquisition company. We are based in Ohio, and we buy real estate anywhere in the United States.

We purchase property directly from owners. Most of the opportunities we look at come to us through direct conversation rather than through a listing, which means the work of the business is largely a matter of talking with owners, understanding what they have, and being straightforward about whether we are the right buyer for it.

We are the decision makers on our own purchases. There is no committee and no approval chain, so we are able to give an owner a clear answer about their property in a reasonable amount of time rather than leaving them waiting on someone else.

How we contact people

When we reach out to a property owner, we may do so by phone, email, or text message. If you have given us your mobile number and agreed to hear from us that way, you can stop those messages at any time by replying STOP, and you can reply HELP to reach us. We do not sell or share the contact information or messaging consent of anyone who hears from us.

Our SMS & Messaging Terms set out exactly what our text messages cover and how to opt out, and our Privacy Policy explains what information we hold and what we do with it.

Contact

The fastest way to reach us is by email. We read everything that comes in, and we answer owners directly.
